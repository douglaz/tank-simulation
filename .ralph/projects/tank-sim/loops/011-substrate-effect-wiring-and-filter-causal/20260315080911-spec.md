# Feature: Substrate Effect Wiring and Filter Causal Integration

## Description
Two structural fields (`detritus_trapping_index`, `grazing_surface_index`) on substrate layers and two hardware fields (`FilterHardware.enabled`, `FilterHardware.flow_lph`) exist but are never consumed by any simulation system. This feature wires them into the appropriate systems so that substrate preset choice and filter configuration produce measurably different simulation outcomes, as required by the prompt.

Specifically:
- **Substrate `detritus_trapping_index`**: Modulates the rate at which particulate detritus breaks down into fine detritus. Higher trapping substrates (coarse porous, active planted) sequester more organics, slowing their release into the water column.
- **Substrate `grazing_surface_index`**: Modulates shrimp access to periphyton and fine detritus food sources. Higher grazing surface substrates make more biofilm accessible to shrimp foraging.
- **`FilterHardware.enabled`**: When disabled, nitrifier performance drops dramatically (nitrifiers lose their primary colonization surface), and filter-contributed gas exchange is zero. Clogging progression only occurs when the filter is running.
- **`FilterHardware.flow_lph`**: Scales nitrifier efficiency (higher flow = better substrate contact) and contributes a small gas exchange boost (water turnover at surface). Also drives progressive clogging accumulation each daily tick.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] `detritus_trapping_index` (averaged across substrate layers, weighted by depth) scales the `feed_leach_rate_per_hour` in the decomposition pathway: higher trapping = slower leaching from particulate to fine detritus (multiplier: `1.0 - 0.5 * avg_trapping_index`, so max 50% reduction)
- [ ] `grazing_surface_index` (averaged across substrate layers, weighted by depth) scales shrimp food access fractions for both periphyton and fine detritus consumption in `shrimp_feeding()`: base rates multiplied by `0.5 + 0.5 * avg_grazing_index` (range 0.5x to 1.0x of current rates)
- [ ] `grazing_surface_index` also modulates periphyton carrying capacity in `algae_growth`: colonizable area is further scaled by `0.7 + 0.3 * avg_grazing_index`
- [ ] When `filter.enabled == false`, nitrifier `combined_env` factor is multiplied by 0.1 (90% reduction — some rock/substrate colonization remains)
- [ ] When `filter.enabled == true`, `flow_lph` modulates nitrifier efficiency: `flow_factor = (flow_lph / volume_l).min(1.0).max(0.1)` applied as a multiplier to nitrifier vmax scaling
- [ ] Filter flow contributes to gas exchange: `filter_kla_boost = if filter.enabled { 0.02 * (flow_lph / 200.0).min(2.0) } else { 0.0 }` added to `k_la` in the DO system
- [ ] Daily tick increments `filter_state.clogging_index` when filter is enabled: `clogging_index += 0.002 * (1.0 - cleanliness_index) * detritus_pressure` where `detritus_pressure = (fine_detritus_g_total / volume_l).min(1.0)`, clamped to [0.0, 1.0]
- [ ] When filter is disabled, `clogging_index` does not increment
- [ ] New test `substrate_trapping_divergence`: Two identical tanks differing only in substrate (`inert_gravel` with trapping=0.2 vs `coarse_porous` with trapping=0.7). After 14 days of identical feeding, the coarse porous tank must have at least 10% more particulate detritus retained and lower peak TAN (due to slower release)
- [ ] New test `substrate_grazing_divergence`: Two identical stocked tanks differing only in substrate grazing index. After 30 days, shrimp condition in the high-grazing substrate must be measurably higher (at least 2% better average condition)
- [ ] New test `filter_enabled_vs_disabled`: Two identical cycling tanks, one with filter enabled and one disabled. After 30 days, the filtered tank must reach lower TAN and nitrite levels, and must have higher biofilter maturity
- [ ] New test `filter_flow_affects_cycling`: Two filtered tanks with different `flow_lph` (50 vs 400 LPH). After 30 days, the higher-flow tank must show faster TAN clearance
- [ ] All existing acceptance tests continue to pass (existing defaults use filter enabled + standard flow, so behavior shifts are within tolerance)
- [ ] Invariants enforced: `clogging_index` clamped [0.0, 1.0], all substrate indices clamped [0.0, 1.0]
