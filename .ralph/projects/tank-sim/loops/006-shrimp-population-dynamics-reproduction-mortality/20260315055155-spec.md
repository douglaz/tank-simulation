# Feature: Shrimp Population Dynamics, Reproduction, Mortality, and Microfauna Turnover

## Description
Loops 1 through 5 already cover the deterministic engine/save-load path, geometry and thermal response, dissolved chemistry/DO/pH/events, nitrifier-driven cycling, and substrate-driven plant/algae competition. The next missing mandatory milestone is activating the currently passive shrimp and microfauna placeholders. This feature turns `AnimalState`, `MicrofaunaState`, `ShrimpPreset`, and shrimp actions into live simulation systems: hourly shrimp stress accumulation, daily condition and molt stress, spawning, egg development, hatching, juvenile recruitment, mortality, and microfauna turnover. It also makes the species pack actually drive behavior, exposes shrimp status through `TankSnapshot`, and preserves deterministic RNG behavior across save/load.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] `shrimp_profile_id` is no longer inert: scenario materialization loads shrimp species parameters into core runtime state or an equivalent deterministic config surface.
- [ ] `ShrimpPreset` is expanded with the minimum fields needed for v1 shrimp dynamics: temperature anchors, mineral/GH anchors, base spawn rate, egg duration, hatch success anchors, juvenile sensitivity, and high-temperature reproductive penalty parameters.
- [ ] An hourly shrimp stress update runs after chemistry/DO updates and before hourly event emission, accumulating NH3, nitrite, low-DO, heat, and chemistry-instability stress into hidden daily state without directly changing populations each hour.
- [ ] A simple hidden stability tracker records recent swings in temperature, pH, GH, and DO, and that tracker feeds daily shrimp condition, molt stress, and reproduction calculations.
- [ ] Daily microfauna turnover updates `population_index` and `grazing_pressure_index` from detritus/periphyton resource availability and shrimp grazing pressure; both indices remain clamped to `0.0..=1.0`.
- [ ] Microfauna modestly improves mineralization efficiency or detritus processing, consumes some periphyton/bacterial resource, and declines under heavy shrimp grazing without driving any mass pool negative.
- [ ] Daily shrimp condition uses periphyton/food availability, DO, NH3, nitrite, temperature, GH, and recent instability; `condition_index` stays in `0.0..=1.0`, and NH3 risk weighs more strongly than TAN alone.
- [ ] Daily molt stress rises under low GH, abrupt chemistry swings, poor condition, and thermal stress, and decays slowly when conditions stabilize.
- [ ] Reproductive readiness is best around `22 C` to `26 C`, clearly worse by `30 C`, and near-zero by `33 C`.
- [ ] Spawning uses the required eligible-female proxy (`0.5 * adults_count - berried_females_count`, clamped at zero) or a documented equivalent, then samples new berried females deterministically from stored RNG using temperature, condition, stability, and mineral factors.
- [ ] Egg development advances daily; successful hatching creates juveniles; failed clutches emit `EggFailure` with non-empty cause codes tied to oxygen, temperature, minerals, or instability.
- [ ] Failure-mode invariant for clutch resolution: on hatch failure, `juveniles_count` does not increase, only the resolved clutch leaves `berried_females_count`, `egg_progress_days` resets only for completed clutches, and no animal count becomes negative.
- [ ] Mortality is probabilistic but deterministic-from-seed, increases with NH3, severe nitrite, low DO, sustained heat, molt stress, and starvation, and can affect juveniles more strongly than adults.
- [ ] Shrimp feeding consumes periphyton as food and can modestly suppress microfauna; this must reuse loop-5 algae/periphyton state rather than introducing a parallel food pool.
- [ ] `apply_action(PlayerAction::RemoveShrimp { count })` becomes state-aware: if `count` exceeds currently removable adults after accounting for earlier queued shrimp actions, return a typed `SimError` and leave state, queued actions, RNG draw count, and event log unchanged.
- [ ] Successful shrimp removals preserve `berried_females_count <= adults_count` and keep all population fields non-negative.
- [ ] The daily pipeline order becomes plants -> algae/periphyton -> microfauna turnover -> shrimp condition/reproduction/hatching/recruitment -> mortality -> biofilter/stability summary.
- [ ] `TankSnapshot` exposes juvenile count, berried female count, shrimp condition, molt stress, reproductive readiness, and microfauna status for the later TUI milestone.
- [ ] `ShrimpBerried`, `EggFailure`, and `MoltStressWarning` are emitted with non-empty `cause_codes`, and tests assert on `kind` and `cause_codes` rather than summary text.
- [ ] Add mandatory acceptance test `thermal_reproduction_penalty`: identical stocked scenarios at `25 C` and `30 C` for `60` days must end with at least `20%` lower reproductive readiness and fewer juveniles in the `30 C` tank.
- [ ] Add deterministic regression coverage for berried transitions, hatch failure, mortality under combined low-DO/high-NH3 stress, microfauna suppression under heavy shrimp grazing, and shrimp-removal validation invariants.
