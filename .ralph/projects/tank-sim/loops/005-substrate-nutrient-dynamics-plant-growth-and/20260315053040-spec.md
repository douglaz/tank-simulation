# Feature: Substrate Nutrient Dynamics, Plant Growth, and Algae/Periphyton Competition

## Description
Activate the currently inert plant guilds, substrate nutrient stores, and algae/periphyton pools by implementing daily growth kinetics, nutrient uptake pathways, and competition mechanics. After this loop, plants grow or decline based on light, temperature, and nutrient availability (water-column vs substrate depending on guild); algae and periphyton compete with plants for light and dissolved nutrients; substrate nutrient stores are consumed by rooted plants; and dying biomass enters the detritus pathway. This corresponds to milestone 5 in the master prompt.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] **Daily plant growth** — each plant guild updates biomass daily using: `net_growth = biomass * max_rate * f_light * f_temp * min(f_n, f_p, f_c) * habitat_factor * (1 - crowding) - respiration - senescence`
- [ ] **Guild-differentiated nutrient uptake** — `FastStem` draws N and P primarily from the water column (`water_column_uptake_bias = 0.9`); `RootFeedingRosette` draws primarily from substrate stores (`substrate_uptake_bias = 0.9`). Uptake removes mass from `ammonia_total_mg_n_total` / `nitrate_mg_n_total` / `phosphate_mg_p_total` (water column) or `nutrient_store_mg_n_total` / `nutrient_store_mg_p_total` (substrate layers).
- [ ] **Substrate nutrient depletion** — rooted plant uptake reduces substrate N and P stores over time; active planted substrate starts with higher stores than inert substrate and supports better rosette growth.
- [ ] **Plant crowding and self-shading** — `crowding_index` is recomputed daily based on total plant biomass relative to tank surface area; higher crowding reduces growth and shades algae.
- [ ] **Plant habitat factor** — `habitat_index` for `RootFeedingRosette` depends on substrate depth and CEC; `FastStem` gets a flat high habitat factor regardless of substrate.
- [ ] **Plant health tracking** — `health_index` degrades under persistent nutrient deficiency or poor light; recovers slowly when conditions improve.
- [ ] **Senescence to detritus** — a fraction of plant biomass lost to senescence and trimming enters `fine_detritus_g_total`.
- [ ] **Daily algae growth** — suspended algae and periphyton grow based on light, temperature, dissolved N and P, reduced by plant shading (crowding) and microfauna grazing.
- [ ] **Periphyton surface limitation** — periphyton growth is capped by total colonizable area from substrate layers plus wall area.
- [ ] **Nuisance index update** — `nuisance_index` recalculated daily from combined algae + periphyton pressure relative to tank size.
- [ ] **Algae event emission** — `AlgaeRiskRising` emitted when nuisance index crosses 0.5; `AlgaeBloom` emitted when suspended algae biomass exceeds a threshold per liter.
- [ ] **Plant process parameters** — new fields added to `ProcessParams` for plant max growth rate, respiration fraction, senescence fraction, nutrient half-saturation constants, light and temperature response curves, and algae growth parameters.
- [ ] **Snapshot exposure** — `TankSnapshot` extended with `suspended_algae_biomass_g`, `periphyton_biomass_g`, `algae_nuisance_index`, per-guild plant biomass summaries, and substrate nutrient remaining.
- [ ] **Acceptance test: `rooted_substrate_advantage`** — same `RootFeedingRosette` in active substrate vs inert sand; after 30 days biomass in active substrate is at least 15% higher.
- [ ] **Acceptance test: `overfeeding_effects` (algae component)** — overfed tank after 14 days has higher algae nuisance index than control.
- [ ] **Integration with hourly DO/DIC pipeline** — photosynthesis and respiration rates already use `photosynthetic_biomass_g()` and `respiring_biomass_g()`, which read current plant/algae biomass; as biomass grows or declines daily, these hourly effects automatically scale. No hourly pipeline change needed for this coupling.
- [ ] **TrimPlants action produces detritus** — trimmed plant mass enters `fine_detritus_g_total` instead of vanishing.
- [ ] **Invariants hold** — plant biomass, algae biomass, substrate nutrients, health/crowding/habitat indices all satisfy existing invariant checks (non-negative, 0..1 for indices).
